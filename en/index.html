About

I am an applied mathematician. For my PhD at ICMC, University of São Paulo, I verified a high-order solver for viscoelastic flows with the method of manufactured solutions: pick an exact solution, compute the source term that makes it solve the problem, and check that the error falls, as the mesh is refined, at the rate the scheme promises. The result was published in 2026 in the International Journal for Numerical Methods in Fluids, open access, and the code and data are public.

Since 2026 I have been a postdoctoral researcher in the Graduate Program in Applied and Computational Mathematics at the State University of Londrina (UEL), supervised by Prof. Paulo Laerte Natti, using the same tool to compare finite differences with neural solvers for partial differential equations. Since May 2026 I have also worked as a data scientist in credit risk at a cooperative financial institution, where I develop, validate and monitor scoring and rating models.

Before that I taught Statistics at the Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar) and Mathematics at the Federal Institute of Goiás (IFG), tutored undergraduates at UNIVESP, and worked at FIPAI on a research partnership with Petrobras, developing and validating scientific software.

Verifying a viscoelastic flow solver

With no analytical solution to compare against, a viscoelastic simulation that looks right may still be solving the wrong equation. The method of manufactured solutions reverses the order: choose the answer, derive the source term that makes it exact, and measure the error on finer and finer meshes. The paper does this for the Oldroyd-B and Giesekus models in a high-order compact finite-difference scheme.

Organista, J.; Souza, L. F.; Fernandes, C.; Costa, W. L.; Silva, A. T. G. da; Castelo, A. Verification of an Incompressible Viscoelastic Flow Solver Using the Method of Manufactured Solutions for Oldroyd-B and Giesekus Constitutive Models. International Journal for Numerical Methods in Fluids, 98(12), 1384-1422, 2026.

CES, a work-hours calculator

A Windows app that tells you when your working day ends and warns you before it does, so you do not slip into overtime. The executable is under 50 KB, needs no installation and never touches the network. The repository includes scripts to audit what the binary can do and to check that it was built from the published source. The interface is in Portuguese.
