Featured research

open access · CC BY 4.0

Verifying a viscoelastic flow solver

The work applies the method of manufactured solutions to verify high-order compact finite-difference schemes for two-dimensional incompressible viscoelastic flows in the vorticity-stream function formulation, with the Oldroyd-B and Giesekus constitutive models. Using the lid-driven cavity as a benchmark, the manufactured solutions retain the original equations and boundary conditions and allow numerical accuracy, convergence and stability to be assessed. The results show a convergence order of about 4.5 for most variables.

Organista, J.; Souza, L. F.; Fernandes, C.; Costa, W. L.; Silva, A. T. G. da; Castelo, A. Verification of an Incompressible Viscoelastic Flow Solver Using the Method of Manufactured Solutions for Oldroyd-B and Giesekus Constitutive Models. International Journal for Numerical Methods in Fluids, 98(12), 1384-1422, 2026.

Personal project · code not public

CNPJ Analytics

A database built on the open data of eight Brazilian federal agencies (Federal Revenue, CVM, Central Bank, SUSEP, CGU, MTE, PGFN and IBGE) that answers what is known about a Brazilian company without any paid lookup, and states the source and date of every figure. It classifies company size through a cascade of evidence (audited revenue, micro-entrepreneur and simplified tax regimes, size declaration and legal form), records which evidence decided and with what confidence, and returns "undetermined" when none is enough.

On the October 2026 national data it processes 70.5 million registrations, opens a company profile in about one second, combines 43 risk signals into a five-level alert and predicts voluntary closure with a model validated on the year after training. More than 2,000 automated tests. Built in spare time.

CES, a work-hours calculator

A Windows app that works out when the working day ends and warns before it does, to avoid overtime. The executable is under 50 KB, needs no installation and never touches the network. The repository includes scripts to audit what the binary can do and to check that it was built from the published source. The interface is in Portuguese.
